# Madhouse Issues
Madhouse Miners Issue Tracker

## Submit Issues
You may open a new Issue at any time if you think you have found a bug or 
other negative experience on the Madhouse Platform and our team will try
to respond without 48 hours.

Please do note that all of our team members are volunteers and do have
a life outside of Madhouse Miners which may result in delays in having
your queries answered.
